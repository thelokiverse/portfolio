02  —  Selected work

Things I’ve shipped.

Four projects I owned end-to-end. Each one is a problem, the calls I made, and what changed in the numbers.
01

Built an AI Assistant from 0→1 for a B2B market-intelligence platform

LLM product · B2B SaaS · 30K MAU

Made it possible to explore proprietary market data through natural-language questions instead of navigating the database manually.

The problem
The platform had a large amount of structured market data, but answering a research question often meant knowing which filters, pages, and fields to use. That worked for specific searches, but was much harder for open-ended questions.
What I built
  • An AI Assistant that could answer questions using the platform’s proprietary data.
  • Worked with engineering, data, and design on the product experience, retrieval scope, response behaviour, and evaluation.
  • After launch, found that discovery was a bigger adoption constraint than answer quality and added entry points across the product.
Impact
~30%
month-3 retention
02

Built AI-assisted workflows for the PM team

AI · Internal product · Workflow

Turned repetitive product work into reusable AI workflows that PMs could use in their day-to-day work.

The problem
PMs were spending time repeatedly doing structured work such as writing requirements and analysing information. The inputs changed, but much of the work involved putting the same pieces together each time.
What I built
  • AI-assisted workflows for recurring PM tasks, with the PM still defining the problem and reviewing the output.
  • Worked with the team to identify useful use cases and iterated based on which workflows became part of the regular process.
Impact
20/20
PMs adopted the workflow
~30–50%
less time on requirements
03

Reworked the first-user experience for a B2B SaaS product

Product · Activation · B2B SaaS

Redesigned the first experience around what a new user needed to understand and do before deciding whether the product was worth paying for.

The problem
The Lite experience served investors, corporates, and sales teams, but the first session did not give users a clear path to the parts of the product most relevant to them.
What I built
  • A more focused first-user experience, including what users saw first, which actions we encouraged, and how we communicated the product’s value before the upgrade point.
  • The work also involved deciding how much of the broader product should be exposed during the first session.
Impact
+15%
first-week product usage
+9%
upgrade requests
04

Increased the share of high-value leads from <5% to ~15%

Growth · Acquisition · Segment mix

Shifted acquisition towards the customer segments that mattered more to the business, rather than optimising purely for signup volume.

The problem
Signup volume was growing, but the mix of customers was not improving at the same rate. Segments such as VC, PE, and investment banking were significantly more valuable than lower-value segments such as universities and founders.
What I built
  • Reworked content and landing pages around six core customer segments, with more emphasis on the segments that converted at higher value.
  • This became part of the broader acquisition surface across SEO landing pages, Lite, the first-user experience, and the home dashboard.
Impact
<5→15%
high-value lead share
